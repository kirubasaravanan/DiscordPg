## How to report an issue

Use `/complaint` in Discord and describe the problem in a sentence or two —
what's wrong and where (e.g. "bathroom tap in room 101 is leaking"). A ticket
is created immediately and you can check its status any time with `/status`.

## Response times

- **Urgent** (no water, no power, security/lock issue): same day.
- **High** (leaks, broken appliances affecting daily use): within 2 days.
- **Medium/Low** (cosmetic issues, minor fixes): within a week, scheduled
  around other work.

Priority is set automatically when you file a ticket; management can adjust
it if needed.

## What to do in an emergency

For anything genuinely dangerous (gas smell, electrical sparking, water
flooding a room) contact management directly by phone immediately — don't
wait on a ticket for those.

## Access for repairs

Maintenance staff will coordinate a time with you before entering your room
for a non-urgent repair. For urgent issues, staff may enter to address the
issue if you can't be reached promptly.

## Common self-serve fixes

- **Wifi drops**: try restarting your device first; if it persists across
  multiple devices, file a complaint — it's likely the router, not you.
- **Minor clogs**: avoid using chemical drain cleaners; file a complaint
  instead so it's fixed properly.

## After a repair

A ticket moves to Resolved once the work is done. If the same issue comes
back, file a new complaint referencing it rather than assuming staff already
know — tickets don't automatically reopen.
