# Sunrise PG — House Rules

_This is starter/example content, not real policy — edit this file directly to
match your PG. No code change or deployment is needed; the backend re-reads it
on restart (see app/services/rules_service.py). Phase 6 will index this same
file for the AI FAQ assistant (RAG), so keep it in plain, factual language._

## Check-in / check-out
- Check-in: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Let management know your arrival time in advance.
- Check-out: by 11:00 AM on your last day.

## Rent
- Rent is due on the 5th of each month.
- A late fee applies after the 10th — see your rent status with `/rent`.
- Payments are recorded by management once received (cash, UPI, or bank transfer).

## Guests
- Visitors are welcome in common areas until 9:00 PM.
- Overnight guests require prior approval from management.

## Quiet hours
- 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM. Please keep noise low for other residents.

## Cleanliness & common areas
- Keep your room and shared spaces tidy.
- Common areas (kitchen, lounge, laundry) are cleaned on a shared schedule — check the notice board.

## Smoking & alcohol
- No smoking inside rooms or common indoor areas.
- Please be respectful of shared spaces if consuming alcohol.

## Maintenance
- Report any issue (plumbing, electrical, wifi, etc.) with `/complaint` as soon as you notice it.
- Urgent issues (no water, no power, security) are prioritized.

## Notice period
- Give at least 30 days' notice before vacating.
- Security deposit refunds are processed after a checkout inspection.

## Contact
- For anything not covered here, contact management directly.
