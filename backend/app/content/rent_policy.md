# Sunrise PG — Rent Policy

_Starter/example content, not real policy — edit this file directly to match
your PG. See app/content/pg_rules.md for how this content is served and
re-indexed._

## Due date

Rent is due on the 5th of each calendar month, for that month (not in advance
and not in arrears).

## Payment methods

Cash, UPI, or bank transfer. Hand cash payments to management directly and
ask for a receipt. Payments are recorded in the system once received — check
your own status any time with `/rent`.

## Late fees

A late fee applies to any balance still unpaid after the 10th of the month.
Contact management if you're going to be late so a plan can be worked out
before the fee applies.

## Partial payments

Partial payments are accepted and recorded against the month's balance —
paying part of what's due still reduces what's outstanding, it isn't treated
as a missed payment.

## Security deposit

A refundable security deposit is collected at move-in. It's held until
checkout, then refunded after a checkout inspection, minus any deductions for
damage beyond normal wear and tear or unpaid dues.

## Rent changes

Any rent change is communicated at least 30 days in advance and never applied
retroactively to a month already underway.

## Refunds

Deposit refunds are processed within 15 business days of a completed checkout
inspection, using the same payment method used for the deposit where possible.
